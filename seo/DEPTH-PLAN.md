# Depth plan - make every page a real guide (Raj feedback, 7 Nov 2026)

Problem: route pages are ~400-600 words with 4 FAQs. Too thin to rank or be quoted by AI answers.

## Standard for every page (target 1,200-2,000 words, 8-12 FAQs)
1. Answer-first summary (distance, time, how to book) 
2. Route at a glance: distance, drive time, road, toll points (measured on Google Maps, dated)
3. Best time to travel, what to avoid (festival crowds, monsoon) - only from official/verified sources
4. Stops on the way (real places, real distances)
5. Sample day plan (clock times only from verified drive times)
6. Which car for which group (existing six-car facts)
7. What to carry / tell us when booking
8. How we price (no fixed fares; quote confirmed directly)
9. 8-12 FAQs with FAQ markup
10. Links to related routes, packages, guides

## Facts needed (ASK Raj; nothing is invented without them)
- Fare rules / sample fares per route (largest gap, biggest ranking win)
- Toll amounts or toll count per route
- Car seat counts and luggage space
- Pickup areas in Solapur, night-travel policy, driver language
- Real customer trips / photos (no fake reviews)
- Temple timings only from official trust sites (checked on the day)
